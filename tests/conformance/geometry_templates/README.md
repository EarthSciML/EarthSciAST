# Geometry templates (`geometry_templates`)

`lib/geometry.esm` is a template-library file (esm-spec §9.7.1) carrying the
pure-AST overlap area of two convex polygons as match-less expression templates:
`overlap_area_planar(ring_a, cell_a, nv_a, ring_b, cell_b, nv_b)` (the
`polygon_intersection_area` leaf with `manifold: "planar"`),
`overlap_area_spherical(...)` (the leaf with `"spherical"` / `"geodesic"`, over
unit-vector stacks, in steradians) and `lonlat_to_unit(ring, cell, vert, comp)`.
This tier checks, in every binding with an inline-test runner and under every
compiler, that the templates applied inside a `faq` over cell pairs give the
right areas.

## The fixtures

| Fixture | Document | What it pins |
|---|---|---|
| `planar_overlap` | `fixtures/planar_overlap.esm` | 4 × 3 cell pairs: identical cells (padded differently), an adjacent pair (shared edge, opposite directions), corner and edge touches, nested and partial overlaps, a clockwise ring, a pentagon in a hexagon slot, and padding by a closing duplicate, trailing repeats and a leading repeat. The template, the template behind the overlap join gate, and the leaf are each asserted exactly (tolerance 0): every coordinate and area is dyadic. |
| `spherical_fan` | `fixtures/spherical_fan.esm` | A meridian fan on the unit sphere: four 15° triangles tiling two 30° triangles along shared great-circle arcs, every ring padded to four slots, the unit-vector stacks built by a `faq` over `lonlat_to_unit`. Nested pairs give `π/12` sr by Girard's theorem (CONFORMANCE_SPEC §5.8.2 band), touching pairs exactly `0`, and each target's row sum `π/6`. |

The goldens are the Julia interpreter's.

## What is not here

- Agreement with the leaf on every polygon and regridding document of the
  repository: `pairs.json` (generated by `scripts/gen-geometry-template-pairs.py`,
  `--check` to verify) lists those cell pairs with exact (planar) or 40-digit
  (spherical) reference areas, and the Julia, Python and Rust test suites run each
  through the template and the leaf (`test/geometry_templates_test.jl`,
  `tests/test_geometry_templates.py`, `tests/geometry_templates.rs`), including a
  padded copy of every pair that must give the same bits.
- A spherical leaf in the document: the leaf's spherical backend is optional in
  Julia (GeometryOps) and Python (spherely).
- The leaf's operand errors: the template does not reject a degenerate ring (a
  ring of no extent gives 0) or a non-convex one (outside its contract).

## Status: not yet in the gate

The tier is not listed in `scripts/test-conformance.sh` or CONFORMANCE_SPEC
§5.45.4 yet, because `native` does not build it in two bindings: Julia
`native` refuses `planar_overlap` (`compiler_refused_rule`: the expanded
template falls through the affine and contraction tiers to a refused per-cell
build) and exhausts memory on `spherical_fan`; Rust `native` refuses
`planar_overlap`'s gated equation (`compiler_refused_rule`: a const-array read
behind a condition its run-time fault scan cannot read). Every binding's
`interpreter`, and Python `native`, pass both documents. It joins the gate once
the native compilers build both.

## Named exclusions

None. Go and TypeScript are scope-excluded: neither has an inline-test runner;
their test suites check the expansion of both documents.
